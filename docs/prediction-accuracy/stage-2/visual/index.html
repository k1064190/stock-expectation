Stage 2

Cuts capital exposure to statistically dead signals by re-weighting the ALGO table and tagging — then filtering — low-edge-band predictions rather than blocking them outright.

Signal Downweight Edge Band

Why

The 30-day weekly calibration run (2026-07-05) grades momentum (24.6%), volume (23.3%), technical (26.5%), and news (21.1%) signals as statistically dead; only sector (52.6%) and fundamental (67.5%) beat a coin flip. Separately, both paper books show the 0.60-0.70 raw-confidence band carrying negative realized edge (US +1.0%, KR -0.1% average round-trip), yet the bulk of predictions log into that band.

What

The /expect ALGO table now weights Momentum +1.5 → +1.0 and Volume +1.0 → +0.5, dropping the ALGO ceiling from 8.0 to 7.0, so a BUY (≥8.0) always requires at least +1.0 of combined news/LLM-context confirmation. A positive LLM_CONTEXT must cite sector relative strength or a fundamental catalyst — never technical or momentum grounds alone. All derived numbers (composite range, threshold rationale, worked examples, sample JSON) were updated to match. predict create now tags LIVE BULL predictions with raw confidence in [0.60, 0.70] as components.low_edge_band = true — a tag, not a block, because a recalibrated-confidence floor is inactionable (isotonic recalibration collapses everything toward ~0.25) and blocking would starve the stage-4 learned blend of training rows. paper_trading_run's new filter_low_edge_band skips tagged predictions before committing paper capital.

How

Development followed TDD: 7 tagging tests and 1 filter test were written first. The tag lives in cmd_predict_create, right after the stage-1 gate refresh; the filter is a pure helper called at the top of run_range, which logs the count of skipped predictions.
