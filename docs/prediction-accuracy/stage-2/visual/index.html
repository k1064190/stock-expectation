Stage 2

Cuts capital exposure to statistically dead signals by re-weighting the ALGO table and tagging — then filtering — low-edge-band predictions rather than blocking them outright.

Signal Downweight Edge Band

Why

The 30-day weekly calibration (2026-07-05) grades momentum (24.6%), volume (23.3%), technical (26.5%), and news (21.1%) as statistically dead; only sector (52.6%) and fundamental (67.5%) beat a coin flip. Separately, both paper books show the 0.60-0.70 raw-confidence band carrying negative realized edge (US +1.0%, KR -0.1% average round-trip), yet the bulk of predictions log there.

What

The /expect ALGO table down-weights Momentum from +1.5 to +1.0 and Volume from +1.0 to +0.5, dropping the ALGO ceiling from 8.0 to 7.0 — so BUY (≥8.0) now always requires at least +1.0 of combined news/LLM-context confirmation, and a positive LLM_CONTEXT must cite sector-RS or a fundamental catalyst rather than technical or momentum grounds alone. All derived numbers (composite range, threshold rationale, worked examples, sample JSON) were updated to match. Separately, predict create now tags LIVE BULL predictions with raw confidence in [0.60, 0.70] as components.low_edge_band = true — tagging rather than blocking, since a recal-confidence floor is inactionable (isotonic collapses everything to ~0.25) and blocking would starve the stage-4 learned blend of training rows. paper_trading_run's filter_low_edge_band then skips tagged predictions before committing paper capital, failing open on absent or unparseable components.

How

TDD: 7 tagging tests and 1 filter test were written first. The tag lives in cmd_predict_create after the stage-1 gate refresh; the filter is a pure helper called at the top of run_range with an info log of the skipped count.
